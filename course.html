Frequently Asked Questions

Is this only for people who are single and dating?

No. This course is for anyone who wants to understand their relationship patterns — whether you're single, dating, or in a relationship wondering "why do I keep feeling this way?"

How is this different from therapy?

Therapy helps you understand your patterns. This course gives you a framework to work with them in real-time — on dates, in texts, in the moments that matter. It's complementary to therapy, not a replacement.

Is this spiritual? Do I have to believe in God?

The word "sacred" means "worthy of devotion." You don't need to believe anything specific. The practices are somatic (body-based) and psychological. The framework works regardless of your beliefs.

How much time do I need each week?

Each module has about 5 lessons (5-10 min each). Most students spend 30-60 minutes per week on lessons plus daily reflection (5-10 min). Go at your own pace — you have lifetime access.

What if it doesn't work for me?

You have 30 days to try the full course. If you do the work and don't see shifts in how you understand your patterns, email us for a complete refund. No questions asked.

What's the difference between the tiers?

Core ($297) gives you the full course. Complete ($397) adds a live Q&A with Marilyn. VIP ($597) adds a private 1:1 clarity call — limited to 10 spots per cohort.
